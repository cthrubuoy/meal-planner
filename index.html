      The shopping list already merges plurals (e.g. "cashew nut" and "cashew nuts"). This goes further and rewrites your saved meals: merges known spellings, makes names singular, fixes mixed units and updates unit defaults. Review first — nothing changes until you press Apply, and you can undo.

        Sync across devices

        Share your meals, photos, plan and shopping list between your tablet, phone and PC — and with your household. Each device keeps its own copy, so the app still works without signal; changes send when you're online.

        Turn on sync on the device with your most up-to-date meals (your tablet). Then link the others with a code.

        What you'll share
        Meals and photos, the week's plan and the shopping list. If you tick off milk, it's ticked for everyone. Your theme and text size stay your own.

        On iPhone: do this in the app on your Home Screen, not in Safari — they keep separate copies. Not installed yet? Settings › Help shows how.

        If this device already has meals, a backup downloads first. Meals on both keep the household's version; meals only here are added for everyone.

        Save your household key now

        If this app's storage is ever cleared, the key brings all your meals back — no other device needed. Save it in your password manager (or copy it somewhere safe).

        Add a device or person

        Show a code here. On the other device, open Meal Planner › Settings › Sync › Join with a code, then tap Scan QR or type the code. (If the app isn't installed there yet, scanning the QR with the camera opens it in the browser.)

        Joining on an iPhone? Add the app to the Home Screen first (Safari › Share › Add to Home Screen), open it from the icon, then type this code — scanning the QR opens Safari, which keeps a separate copy.

            How do I…
            
      
            
        
            Add a meal?
            Tap ＋ Add. Scan a recipe card with the camera, paste a recipe link, or type it in.
            
        
            Plan the week?
            Plan › Auto-fill picks from your own meals (favourites and good ratings first, quicker ones Mon–Thu). Tap a day to choose or swap.
            
        
            Make the shopping list?
            Tick meals with their round ＋ button, or Plan › Add to list. Add anything else (milk, bin bags) at the bottom of the list.
            
        
            Shop?
            Tap an item (or swipe it right) when it's in the trolley. Copy for Ocado copies the names for Ocado's "Find a list of products".
            
        
            Cook?
            Today › Cook. One step at a time in big text, with timers named after what they're timing.
            
        
            Share with someone?
            Settings › Sync › Show a code on your device, then Join with a code on theirs. You share meals, the plan and the shopping list; theme and text size stay your own.
            
      
            

      
            Known limitations on iPhone and iPad
            
      
              
        
            • Install it first. In a Safari tab, Safari can clear the app's data after about a week without a visit. On the Home Screen it's kept.
              • 
        
            • The invite QR code opens Safari, not the app. The app and Safari keep separate copies, so type the code into the app instead (Settings › Sync › Join with a code). The app can't scan QR codes itself on iPhone.
              • 
        
            • Cook-mode timers may not ring when the app is in the background or the screen is locked. Keep it open on the timer (the screen stays on in cook mode).
              • 
        
            • No vibration when you tick things off.
              • 
        
            • You can't share a link into the app. Copy the recipe link, then paste it into ＋ Add › Import from a link.
